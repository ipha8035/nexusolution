The Concept Of
Appropriate Technology

Appropriate technology starts with people. It brings together local knowledge, accessible materials, and practical design to solve everyday challenges.

Solutions should be affordable, easy to maintain, and suited to the communities that use them.

Small ideas. Meaningful change.

Technology makes a difference when it works in the real world. We collect ideas that help communities meet essential needs, make better use of resources, and build a more sustainable future.

Behind every solution is a community.

Explore the knowledge, skills, and everyday ingenuity that make appropriate technology possible.
